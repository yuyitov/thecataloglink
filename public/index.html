FAQ

Straight answers

No inflated promises — here's what The Catalog Link does and doesn't do.

I don't have a catalog link yet. How do I get one?

Two ways, and both let you see it before you pay. Leave us your Instagram handle and we'll build your catalog from your posts and send it to you — that's how everyone else got theirs. Or start from scratch: buy it and send us your products in the form, with your own photos and prices.

Where did my photos and prices come from?

From your own public Instagram posts — the same ones anyone can see today. We didn't get into anything private and you didn't have to give us anything. If a price wasn't posted, your catalog says “Ask for price” instead of a number.

Is it really mine once I pay?

Yes. Your catalog gets its own link and its own QR code, and it stays published. You're not renting it and there's nothing to cancel.

Something is wrong — a price changed, or a product isn't mine anymore.

That's what the 2 included changes are for. You get a personal link, you tell us what to fix, and we republish it. After those two, a small fix is $6.

Can I add products later?

Yes. Small additions count as a $6 extra change. For a bigger batch of new products, message us and we'll quote it on the spot.

Is this an online store? Can people pay on it?

No, and it isn't trying to be. There's no cart, no checkout, no inventory and no shipping. Your catalog shows what you sell and sends the person straight to you — you close the sale and collect the way you do today.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. One payment, once. There is no subscription to cancel and nothing expires.

Do I need an app, or an account?

Neither you nor your customers download or sign up for anything. It opens in any browser, on any phone.

Most of my products don't have a public price. Is that a problem?

Not at all — it's the normal case in this line of work. Those products show an “Ask for price” tag, which is a deliberate invitation to write to you, not a blank space. If you'd rather show prices, send them and we put them in.

Can I pick my own colors?

You pick from 12 styles — 3 layouts with 4 palettes each. We start you on the one that fits your products, and switching to another is a normal use of one of your included changes. There's no free-form color picker; that's how we keep every catalog looking right.

What if my customers speak English? Or Spanish?

Every catalog is bilingual automatically. Each person sees it in their language, from the same link — including the category filters.

How many products fit?

Up to 20 with a photo, which is what fits comfortably in a catalog people actually scroll. If you have more, we start with your best ones and you add the rest later.

What if I don't love it?

First we fix it for free — if something is off on our end, we correct it at no cost. If you still don't love it, you get your money back.
